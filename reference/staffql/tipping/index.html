Tipping

The tipping module’s root lookups.

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
tipping IAudienceTipping! The instance’s IAudienceTipping mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
confirmTip
input: TipConfirmInput!		 TipConfirmPayload Checks a tip payment with its provider after the app finishes paying, before the webhook arrives. requires tipping:write:self
connectPersonSquare staffql only
input: PersonSquareConnectInput!		 PersonSquareConnectPayload Starts connecting a performer’s own Square account so fans can tip them in the apps. Send them to authorizeUrl; Square brings them back to returnTo with square=connected, pick_location (pick one with setPersonSquareLocation), declined or error. requires tipping:write:mine
connectPersonStripe staffql only
input: PersonStripeConnectInput!		 PersonStripeConnectPayload Starts connecting a performer’s own Stripe account so fans can tip them in the apps. Send them to onboardingUrl; Stripe brings them back to returnTo with stripe=connected (or pending, or error). requires tipping:write:mine
payTip
input: TipPayInput!		 TipPayPayload Pays a tip to a performer who takes Square with a payment token from Square’s In-App Payments SDK (a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), after startTip. requires tipping:write:self
refundTip staffql only
input: TipRefundInput!		 TipRefundPayload Refunds a paid tip from the performer’s account. Admins only. requires tipping:write:*
setPersonSquareLocation staffql only
input: PersonSquareLocationSetInput!		 PersonSquareLocationSetPayload Picks which of a performer’s Square locations their tips are paid to. requires tipping:write:mine
startTip
input: TipStartInput!		 TipStartPayload Starts tipping a performer in the app, through their own Stripe or Square account (the IN_APP option of their tipping mixin). Stripe: finish with Stripe’s payment sheet (paymentIntentClientSecret, on their account stripeAccountId, with publishableKey), then call confirmTip. Square: make a payment token with Square’s In-App Payments SDK (squareApplicationId, squareLocationId), then call payTip with it. Asking again for the same amount soon after returns the same tip. requires tipping:write:self

Enums

Section titled “Enums”

TipProvider

Section titled “TipProvider”

Enum

A way to tip a performer (a Tipping provider).

  • CASHAPP A link to their Cash App $cashtag.
  • IN_APP Paid in the app through their own Stripe or Square account: startTip, then payTip (Square) or confirmTip (Stripe).
  • PAYPAL A link to their PayPal.Me page.
  • VENMO A link to their Venmo.
