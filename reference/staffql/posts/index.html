Posts

The posts module’s root lookups. A venue’s posts are on it (Venue.posts).

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
posts IAudiencePosts! The instance’s IAudiencePosts mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
addEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoTakeInput!		 EventPhotoTakePayload Adds a photo taken with the app’s camera at a venue (only while the app places you there): it’s marked AMATEUR, credited to you, put on the event under way there, and tagged with whoever is on stage, so it shows on the venue’s, the event’s and their pages (IPosts.photos). requires posts:write:self
createPost staffql only
input: PostCreateInput!		 PostCreatePayload Posts as a venue on the venue, one of its events or one of its series; or, with asPersonId, as a person (talent) on their own page or an event they’re on. Name exactly one of venueId, eventId, eventSeriesId and personId. requires posts:write:mine or posts:write:my_troupes
createPostComment
input: PostCommentCreateInput!		 PostCommentCreatePayload Comments on a venue’s or a person’s post, as the signed-in user. Someone banned from the venue’s chat can’t, and nobody can once its comments are closed. requires posts:write:self
deletePost staffql only
input: PostDeleteInput!		 PostDeletePayload Removes a venue’s or a person’s post and its comments from the apps. requires posts:write:mine or posts:write:my_troupes
hidePostFromEvent staffql only
input: PostHideFromEventInput!		 PostHideFromEventPayload Hides a talent’s post from its event, or shows it again: for the event venue’s managers and admins. requires posts:write:mine
mutePostAuthor
input: PostAuthorMuteInput!		 PostAuthorMutePayload Hides a venue’s or a person’s posts from the signed-in user, or shows them again. Name exactly one of venueId and personId. requires posts:write:self
removeEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoRemoveInput!		 EventPhotoRemovePayload Removes a photo you took with the app’s camera (addEventPhoto), from every page it was on. requires posts:write:self
removePostComment
input: PostCommentRemoveInput!		 PostCommentRemovePayload Removes a comment on a post: its author’s own, or any on a post for whoever manages its poster (the venue’s managers, the talent listing’s) and admins. requires posts:write:self or posts:moderate:mine
updatePost staffql only
input: PostUpdateInput!		 PostUpdatePayload Changes a venue’s or a person’s post. Fields left out aren’t changed. requires posts:write:mine or posts:write:my_troupes

Post

Section titled “Post”

Object · implements HasTimestamps, Node

A post made as a venue or as a person (a talent listing): on the venue, one of its events or series, or the person’s own page. Shown as its poster.

Field Type Description
actingPersonName String On a troupe’s post, the member who wrote it for the troupe (“Kiki Riot for House of Lux”).
body String! What it says.
commentCount Int! How many comments it has.
comments
after: String
before: String
first: Int
last: Int		 PostCommentConnection! Its comments, oldest first.
commentsOpen Boolean! Whether people can comment on it.
createdAt ISO8601DateTime! When it was posted.
edited Boolean! Whether its text changed after it was posted.
event Event The event it’s on, if it’s on one.
eventSeries EventSeries The series it’s on, if it’s on one.
hiddenFromEvent Boolean! Whether the event’s venue hid this talent post from the event. It still shows on the person’s page.
id ID! Its UUID.
person Person The talent listing posting it, when a person posted.
photoUrl PhotoURL Its first photo.
photos [PostPhoto!]! Its photos, in order: at most four.
pinned Boolean! Whether it is pinned above the others.
posterAvatarUrl PhotoURL Its poster’s picture.
posterName String! Who it shows as: the venue or the person, in full.
roleLabel String The tag under the poster’s name: “Venue” for a venue; for talent on an event, their part (“MC”, “DJ”, “Act”, “Duet”, “Troupe”, “Host”); else null.
updatedAt ISO8601DateTime! When it last changed.
venue Venue The venue posting it; on a talent post, its event’s venue. Null on a talent post on their own page.
viewerCanHideFromEvent Boolean! Whether the signed-in user may hide this talent post from its event: the event venue’s managers and admins.
viewerCanManage Boolean! Whether the signed-in user may change it: whoever manages its poster (the venue, or the talent listing) and admins.
visibility PostVisibility! Who can see it: members in the apps, or the web too.

id ID! Its UUID.

Section titled “PostsView”

Field Type Description

A venue’s own posts (as the venue, on it, its events and series); an event’s by its venue and the people on it; a series’; a person’s own; a locale’s updates from the last 14 days. Enums

Section titled “Enums”

EventPhotoSource

Section titled “EventPhotoSource”

Enum

Who took a photo of a venue or event (EventPhoto).

  • AMATEUR A member’s, taken with the app’s camera there.
  • PROFESSIONAL A photographer’s, published from hedonism_bot.

PostsFrom

Section titled “PostsFrom”

Enum

Whose posts a posts list shows (IPosts.list).

  • ALL Everyone.
  • GOING Posts on events the signed-in viewer is going to; none when they’re signed out.
  • TALENT Posts made as people (talent listings).
  • VENUES Posts made as venues.

PostVisibility

Section titled “PostVisibility”

Enum

Who can see a post: members in the apps, or the whole internet too. Comments are never public.

  • COMMUNITY Anyone signed in to the apps. The default.
  • PUBLIC The web too: its page on the audience’s site, share cards and search engines, while its poster is public as well.
