Events

An audience’s events and the recurring series they come from, and lookups of one by its UUID.

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
events IAudienceEvents! The instance’s IAudienceEvents mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
createEvent staffql only
input: EventCreateInput!		 EventCreatePayload Adds an event at one of the audience’s venues. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes
createEventSeries staffql only
input: EventSeriesCreateInput!		 EventSeriesCreatePayload Adds a recurring series run by one of the audience’s venues or people, and puts its occurrences on the calendar. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
deleteEvent staffql only
input: EventDeleteInput!		 EventDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s events, with its RSVPs. Canceling it keeps it listed instead. Deleting an occurrence of a series skips its date. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes
deleteEventSeries staffql only
input: EventSeriesDeleteInput!		 EventSeriesDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s recurring series with its upcoming occurrences, except ones edited on their own or that people have RSVPed to. Past occurrences stay as ordinary events. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes
deleteTriviaSet staffql only
input: TriviaSetDeleteInput!		 TriviaSetDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s own trivia sets. Segments set to it pick one by their tags again; rounds that ran keep their tallies. requires events:write:venue or people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes
grantEventAccess staffql only
input: EventAccessGrantInput!		 EventAccessGrantPayload Grants an event to a talent listing that isn’t hosting it or on its lineup, like crew or a photographer: it’s among the listing’s events in Backstage, and its managers edit the event’s lineup and are in its back-of-house room. Its venue’s managers, the talent who listed it, its hosts’ managers and the audience’s admins grant it. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
importChatPrompts staffql only
input: ChatPromptsImportInput!		 ChatPromptsImportPayload Imports an audiencekit.chatPrompts file (doc/IMPORT_EXPORT.md) onto an event: its prompts replace the event’s that haven’t shown yet, and its settings (unless settings is false) replace the event’s. A file from Event.chat.file or a starter set’s (Event.chat.starterSets.file) both work. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
importTriviaSet staffql only
input: TriviaSetImportInput!		 TriviaSetImportPayload Uploads an audiencekit.triviaSet file (doc/IMPORT_EXPORT.md; Venue.trivia.setFile) as one of the audience’s trivia sets, for Venue.trivia.sets. A key the audience already has is replaced; a built-in set’s key is saved as <key>_custom. requires events:write:venue or people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes
removeChatPrompt staffql only
input: ChatPromptRemoveInput!		 ChatPromptRemovePayload Removes one of an event’s chat prompts, for whoever runs the night: one still to show comes off the list (skipping it); one that’s up is taken out of the room for everyone, and stays in the list as shown (takenDown), so it never goes up again. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
revokeEventAccess staffql only
input: EventAccessRevokeInput!		 EventAccessRevokePayload Takes back an event granted to a talent listing (grantEventAccess). Whoever may grant it revokes it. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
setEventChatPrompts staffql only
input: EventChatPromptsSetInput!		 EventChatPromptsSetPayload Changes an event’s chat prompts (Event.chat): whether and when its room shows them, and the list itself. A list replaces the one it has, in its order; prompts already shown tonight stay as they were, whatever the list says. starterSet starts the list from one of Event.chat.starterSets. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
showNextChatPrompt staffql only
input: ChatPromptShowNextInput!		 ChatPromptShowNextPayload Puts an event’s next chat prompt up in its venue’s room now, pinned under the title, without waiting for the rotation: for whoever runs the night (Velvet, Backstage, Focus). It goes up even past the nightly cap, while an act is on, or with prompts off; the rotation counts on from it. Name the event (id), which must be on now, or the venue (venueId) for the latest event on there now with prompts left. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
updateEvent staffql only
input: EventUpdateInput!		 EventUpdatePayload Changes one of the audience’s events. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes
updateEventSeries staffql only
input: EventSeriesUpdateInput!		 EventSeriesUpdatePayload Changes one of the audience’s recurring series. Its upcoming occurrences follow, except ones edited on their own. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue

Event

Section titled “Event”

Object · implements HasBanner, HasTags, HasTaskTemplates, Node

An event hosted at a venue or awaiting a venue.

Field Type Description
accessGrants staffql only [AccessGrant!] The talent listings granted it without being on it, oldest first (grantEventAccess). Null for anyone who may not manage them: only its venue’s managers, the talent who listed it, its hosts and the audience’s admins see them.
bannerPhotoId ID Identifier of the event’s banner photo.
bannerUrl PhotoURL Its event or series photo, else a host photo, else its venue hero photo.
chat IEventChat The instance’s IEventChat mixin, when available to the viewer. requires events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue
description String Description of the event.
endAt ISO8601DateTime When the event ends.
eventSeries EventSeries The recurring series it is an occurrence of.
facebookId String Its Facebook event’s ID. A string: Facebook IDs don’t fit GraphQL’s Int.
facets [String!]! The keys of the facets switched on for it, e.g. karaoke, in key order: each is a tag the system keeps (Tag.facet) and turns on the edges of the mixins declaring it (@mixin(tag:)). Only those whose module is installed.
id ID! Unique identifier.
isCanceled Boolean! Whether the event has been canceled.
isFeatured Boolean! Whether the event is featured.
karaoke IKaraokeNight The instance’s IKaraokeNight mixin, when available. Null unless it has the karaoke facet (its facets).
locale Locale Its venue’s locale, or its series’ while its venue is still to be announced.
name String The display name: the override when one is set, otherwise the name.
nameOverride String Display name set for this occurrence only.
occurrenceOn ISO8601Date The series date it stands for, even when it’s been moved.
order Int! Display ordering value.
people IEventPeople! The instance’s IEventPeople mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
photography IEventPhotos The instance’s IEventPhotos mixin, when available.
ping IPingFeed The instance’s IPingFeed mixin, when available.
posterArt staffql only [PosterArt!]! Its generated backgrounds and commissioned posters, newest first. Whoever may change it sees them all; everyone else, the READY ones, which are made to be shared (Backstage).
posts IPosts! The instance’s IPosts mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
roomEnabled staffql only Boolean Whether it has a back-of-house chat by its own setting; null follows its series’ (EventSeries.roomEnabled), on without one.
roomOn staffql only Boolean! Whether it has a back-of-house chat, its own setting or its series’.
rooms staffql only IRooms The instance’s IRooms mixin, when available to the viewer. requires rooms:read:member
rsvp IEventRSVP! The instance’s IEventRSVP mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
seriesOverride Boolean! An occurrence edited on its own: changes to its series leave it alone.
sourceUrl String Where it was found, for imported events.
startAt ISO8601DateTime When the event starts.
tags [Tag!]! Its audience tags, including tags connected to module facets, by name.
taskTemplates [TaskTemplate!]! Its lineup task templates, in order. Empty for anyone but whoever keeps them (its managers; an event’s hosts too).
tickets IEventTickets! The instance’s IEventTickets mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
venue IEventVenue! The instance’s IEventVenue mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
venueOpenOn ISO8601Date The local date when it starts at venue opening, or null for an explicit time.
viewerRsvp RSVPState! The signed-in user’s RSVP. Shipped Hot Mess apps still read it; the rsvp module answers it.
visibility ListingVisibility! Whether full listing details are public on the web.

EventSeries

Section titled “EventSeries”

Object · implements HasBanner, HasTags, HasTaskTemplates, Node

A recurring event, such as “first Friday of the month”. Its occurrences are ordinary events, put on the calendar 90 days ahead.

Field Type Description
bannerUrl PhotoURL Its banner, which its occurrences show until one has its own.
description String What it is, copied to each occurrence.
durationMinutes Int How long each occurrence runs. Null: until close.
events [Event!]! Its upcoming occurrences, soonest first.
facets [String!]! The keys of the facets switched on for it, e.g. karaoke, in key order: each is a tag the system keeps (Tag.facet) and turns on the edges of the mixins declaring it (@mixin(tag:)). Only those whose module is installed.
generatedThrough ISO8601Date The last day occurrences were put on the calendar through.
id ID! Its UUID.
karaoke Boolean! Whether its occurrences are karaoke nights.
karaokeSessionCode String Its session code. Null means venue controlled.
locale Locale Where it happens: its venue’s locale, or the one chosen while its venue is to be announced.
name String! Its name, copied to each occurrence.
owner EventSeriesOwner! The venue or person who runs it.
people IEventSeriesPeople! The instance’s IEventSeriesPeople mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
posts IPosts! The instance’s IPosts mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
recurrenceText String Its rule in words, e.g. “Every month on the 1st Friday”.
roomEnabled Boolean! Whether its occurrences have a back-of-house chat. An occurrence can say otherwise (Event.roomEnabled).
roomsAhead Int! How many of its upcoming occurrences have their back-of-house chat listed among the rooms people are in (Audience.rooms), 2 unless changed. One further ahead opens when asked for.
rrule String! Its RFC 5545 recurrence rule, e.g. FREQ=MONTHLY;BYDAY=1FR.
skippedDates [ISO8601Date!]! Dates the rule produces that it skips.
startTime TimeOfDay! When each occurrence starts, local time.
startsOn ISO8601Date! The first date the rule counts from.
tags [Tag!]! Its audience tags, including tags connected to module facets, by name.
taskTemplates [TaskTemplate!]! Its lineup task templates, in order. Empty for anyone but whoever keeps them (its managers; an event’s hosts too).
tickets IEventSeriesTickets! The instance’s IEventSeriesTickets mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
timeZone String! The IANA time zone start_time is in.
upcomingStarts
count: Int		 [ISO8601DateTime!]! When the next occurrences start, skipped dates left out.
venue IEventSeriesVenue! The instance’s IEventSeriesVenue mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
