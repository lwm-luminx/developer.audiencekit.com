Venues

An audience’s venues, a lookup of one by its UUID, and a venue’s staff devices.

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
onboardingInvites staffql only
includeEnded: Boolean
subjectId: ID!		 [OnboardingInvite!]! A venue’s or performer’s onboarding invites (createOnboardingInvite), newest first: the pending ones, or every one with includeEnded. For the people who manage it only. requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or audience:admin:*
venues IAudienceVenues! The instance’s IAudienceVenues mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
addPosterArtwork staffql only
input: PosterArtworkAddInput!		 PosterArtworkAddPayload Adds a commissioned poster an artist made for a venue, event or performer. It overrides the generated poster wherever it is set. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue or people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes
addVenueBannerPhoto staffql only
input: VenueBannerPhotoAddInput!		 VenueBannerPhotoAddPayload Adds an image to the end of a venue’s banner images: uploaded, or one of its photographers’ photos. requires venues:write:mine
answerSubmission staffql only
input: SubmissionVenueAnswerInput!		 SubmissionVenueAnswerPayload Answers a reviewer’s question about a suggestion for one of your venues: whether it is right. A reviewer still decides. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue
convertVenueToPerson staffql only
input: VenueConvertToPersonInput!		 VenueConvertToPersonPayload Converts a venue (such as a pop-up party filed as a location) into a person in this audience, or folds it into an existing person: its Facebook Page, website, photos, social links, tags, posts, access, events and recurring series move to the person, featured in the venue’s locale, and the venue is deleted. All or nothing. requires venues:write:*
createOnboardingInvite staffql only
input: OnboardingInviteCreateInput!		 OnboardingInviteCreatePayload Makes a QR invite that onboards one person into Velvet (a venue) or Backstage (a performer) through its App Clip. Show invite.url as a QR code; the code is also in invite.code. requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or audience:admin:*
createStageZone staffql only
input: StageZoneCreateInput!		 StageZoneCreatePayload Adds a zone to a venue (“+ Add zone”), e.g. the patio: it runs its own night lineup. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:venue
createVenue staffql only
input: VenueCreateInput!		 VenueCreatePayload Adds a venue to the audience: a bar, club or other place that holds events. requires venues:write:* or locales:admin:mine
deleteStageZone staffql only
input: StageZoneDeleteInput!		 StageZoneDeletePayload Removes a venue’s zone. Its lineups go, and their entries fall back to the venue’s default zone (an event’s to its default-zone lineup). Its support staff then work every zone. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:venue
deleteVenue staffql only
input: VenueDeleteInput!		 VenueDeletePayload Deletes one of the audience’s venues and its events. Hiding it keeps them instead. requires venues:write:*
generatePosterBackground staffql only
input: PosterBackgroundGenerateInput!		 PosterBackgroundGeneratePayload Asks the image model for a new text-free poster background for a venue, event or performer, flavoured to them (PosterBackground). It answers at once with status GENERATING; poll posterArt until it is READY (up to a minute). requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue or people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes
grantObjectAccess staffql only
input: AccessGrantCreateInput!		 AccessGrantCreatePayload Grants access on a subject (Venue, Person, Locale) to a user with an action (admin, write, read). requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or locales:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or locales:admin:* or audience:admin:*
hideVenueRegularNight staffql only
input: VenueRegularNightHideInput!		 VenueRegularNightHidePayload Hides one of a venue’s regular nights from its tag layers, or shows it again. It stays hidden until it fades out; to keep a tag for good, add it to the venue’s own tags instead. For its managers and the audience’s admins. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue
issueVenueDevice staffql only
input: VenueDeviceIssueInput!		 VenueDeviceIssuePayload Issues a one-time device code with permissions for this venue. requires venues:admin:mine or venues:admin:venue
mergeVenue staffql only
input: VenueMergeInput!		 VenueMergePayload Merges a duplicate venue into another venue in this audience: its events, series, chat and rooms, posts, tags, staff, access, photos and Pages move to the venue kept, which fills its blank details from it, and the duplicate is deleted. All or nothing. requires venues:write:*
removePosterArt staffql only
input: PosterArtRemoveInput!		 PosterArtRemovePayload Removes a generated background or a commissioned poster. Posters already downloaded keep it. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:mine or events:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue or people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes
removeVenueBannerPhoto staffql only
input: VenueBannerPhotoRemoveInput!		 VenueBannerPhotoRemovePayload Takes an image out of a venue’s banner images. requires venues:write:mine
renewOnboardingInvite staffql only
input: OnboardingInviteRenewInput!		 OnboardingInviteRenewPayload Gives a pending or expired invite a new code and expiry; the old code stops working. requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or audience:admin:*
reorderVenueBannerPhotos staffql only
input: VenueBannerPhotosReorderInput!		 VenueBannerPhotosReorderPayload Puts a venue’s banner images in this order. Name each of them once. requires venues:write:mine
revokeObjectAccess staffql only
input: AccessGrantRevokeInput!		 AccessGrantRevokePayload Revokes an access grant from a subject. requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or locales:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or locales:admin:* or audience:admin:*
revokeOnboardingInvite staffql only
input: OnboardingInviteRevokeInput!		 OnboardingInviteRevokePayload Stops a pending invite working. Access it already granted stays; remove that on the access list. requires venues:admin:mine or people:admin:mine or venues:admin:* or people:admin:* or audience:admin:*
revokeVenueDevice staffql only
input: VenueDeviceRevokeInput!		 VenueDeviceRevokePayload Revokes a venue device and its unused code. requires venues:admin:mine or venues:admin:venue
submitLocalRulesCorrection staffql only
input: LocalRulesCorrectionSubmitInput!		 LocalRulesCorrectionSubmitPayload Suggests a correction to a venue’s local rules, as its verified staff. A reviewer decides; age policy and licence class changes need a link or photo, and the public licence record outranks the venue’s claim. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue
updateStageZone staffql only
input: StageZoneUpdateInput!		 StageZoneUpdatePayload Renames, moves or makes default a venue’s zone. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or events:write:venue
updateVenue staffql only
input: VenueUpdateInput!		 VenueUpdatePayload Changes one of the audience’s venues. Listing managers change only the name, description, website, social links and cover of their own. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue or locales:admin:mine

Section titled “OnboardingInvite”

Object · staffql only

A QR invite that onboards one person into Velvet (a venue’s team) or Backstage (a performer’s page) through its App Clip (doc/ONBOARDING_INVITES.md). Single use, expiring and revocable.

Field Type Description
acceptedAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime When it was accepted.
access staffql only String! What accepting grants: owner, manager or staff (Velvet); manager (Backstage).
app staffql only String! velvet or backstage.
claimedAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime When the invitee signed in with it.
claimedByName staffql only String Who signed in with it, as “First L.”, until it is accepted or ends.
code staffql only String The code as people type it (K7QX-2M9P-H4TD), while it is pending.
createdAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime! When it was made.
createdByName staffql only String Who made it, as “First L.”.
expiresAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime! When it stops working.
firstScannedAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime When someone first opened it.
id staffql only ID! Its UUID.
lastScannedAt staffql only ISO8601DateTime When someone last opened it.
note staffql only String The curator’s message to the invitee.
status staffql only String! pending, claimed, accepted, expired or revoked.
subjectId staffql only ID! The venue or person (performer) it joins.
subjectType staffql only String! Venue or Person.
url staffql only String What the QR code opens (https://audiencekit.com/v/… or /b/…), while it is pending.

SafetyShift

Section titled “SafetyShift”

Object · staffql only

Whether the caller’s device hears this venue’s safety alerts, and the name guests see.

Field Type Description
geofenced staffql only Boolean! Whether it hears them only while it reports being inside the venue.
hearing staffql only Boolean! Whether it hears them right now: on shift, and inside the venue when geofenced.

Submission

Section titled “Submission”

Object

A suggestion about the audience’s listings: a missing venue or event, a correction, a closure, a tag, local rules or a performer. Nothing it proposes is live until a reviewer approves it.

Field Type Description
body String What the submitter wrote. Admins only, since it may say who they are. requires audience:moderate:*
choices [String!]! For SOCIAL_LINK: the handles it found, the proposed one first. A reviewer approves another with EDIT_APPROVE and {provider, handle}.
createdAt ISO8601DateTime! When it was filed.
current JSON! The listing as it is now, for the fields it proposes.
decidedAt ISO8601DateTime When it was decided.
decisions [SubmissionDecision!]! Its decisions, oldest first.
disputeCount Int! How many people say it is wrong, e.g. that a venue is still open. requires audience:moderate:*
event Event The event it is about, or that approving it added.
evidence JSON! Links and photos: [{kind, url, photoId, photoUrl}]. Admins only, since they may show who filed it. requires audience:moderate:*
id ID! Its ID.
kind SubmissionKind! What it proposes.
locale Locale The city it was filed in.
person Person The person it is about (SOCIAL_LINK).
priority Float! Its place in the queue: higher first. Admins only. requires audience:moderate:*
proposed JSON! The fields it proposes.
reporterCount Int! How many people filed it.
research [ResearchResult!]! What researching it found, oldest first. Admins only. requires audience:moderate:*
risk String! low, normal or high.
series EventSeries The recurring night it is about.
status SubmissionStatus! Where it is in review.
submitter Profile Who filed it. Admins only. requires audience:moderate:*
submitterRole String! attendee, talent, venue_staff, admin or system, when filed. Admins only. requires audience:moderate:*
submitterTrust Float The submitter’s trust when filed, 0 to 1. Admins only. requires audience:moderate:*
venue Venue The venue it is about (or its event is at), or that approving it added.

A decision on a suggestion.

Field Type Description

Venue

Section titled “Venue”

Object · implements HasBanner, HasPhoto, HasTags, HasTaskTemplates, HasTimestamps, Node

A venue in the audience.

Field Type Description
accessGrants staffql only [AccessGrant!]! Permissions granted on this venue. requires venues:read:mine or venues:admin:*
activity VenueActivity How busy it is right now, approximately. Admins and its managers only. requires venues:read:mine or venues:read:venue
adultVenue Boolean! Whether it is flagged for adult content: its posters and TV marquee may use the adult-venue slogan lines.
archived Boolean! Whether it closed for good: kept for its history, listed nowhere.
bannerPhotos [BannerPhoto!]! Its banner images, in the order the apps rotate through.
bannerUrl PhotoURL Its Google place photo when available, else its first banner image, own photo or Facebook Page’s cover photo.
canSendSafetyAlert Boolean! Whether the viewer can send a safety alert here now (sendSafetyAlert): the app places them at the venue. The apps show “Get help” only then.
chat IVenueChat! The instance’s IVenueChat mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
chatRetentionMinutes Int! How many minutes its chat room shows a message before it expires: its own window, else the default.
chatRetentionOverridden Boolean! Whether the venue set its own chat retention window.
contributionQuestions staffql only [Submission!]! Suggestions a reviewer asked the venue’s staff to confirm (answerSubmission). Who suggested them is never shown. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue
createdAt ISO8601DateTime! When the record was created.
description String Its public description.
distance Float Metres from the position the venues were looked up near.
distanceTolerance Int! Distance in metres counted as being at the venue.
editHistory staffql only
limit: Int		 [RecordEdit!]! Changes to its details, newest first: what, when and through what. requires venues:write:mine or venues:write:venue
events IVenueEvents! The instance’s IVenueEvents mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
facets [String!]! The keys of the facets switched on for it, e.g. karaoke, in key order: each is a tag the system keeps (Tag.facet) and turns on the edges of the mixins declaring it (@mixin(tag:)). Only those whose module is installed.
friends IVenueFriends! The instance’s IVenueFriends mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
hidden Boolean! Whether the venue is hidden.
id ID! Its UUID.
karaoke IVenueKaraoke The instance’s IVenueKaraoke mixin, when available. Null unless it has the karaoke facet (its facets).
listed Boolean! False for a community space (its Community space tag), like a pride center that hosts events: listed after the venues to go out to and left out of nearby (doc/ORGANIZERS.md).
localRules VenueLocalRules Its own rules (age policy, licence, last call), as approved; null when none were.
locale Locale! The city or area containing this venue.
location Location Its geographic location.
marqueeSlogans Boolean! Whether its TV marquee (Velvet, the web sign at hotmess.social/venues/<id>/display) shows Hot Mess slogan slides between the lineup: on unless the venue turns them off.
music IVenueMusic! The instance’s IVenueMusic mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
name String The venue’s name, or its Facebook page’s.
order Int! Its display order.
ordering IVenueOrdering The instance’s IVenueOrdering mixin, when available. Null unless it has the ordering facet (its facets).
pairUp IVenuePairUp The instance’s IVenuePairUp mixin, when available. Null unless it has the pair_up facet (its facets).
pairUpEnabled Boolean! Whether Quickie is on there (Venue.pairUp): on unless the venue opted out.
people IVenuePeople! The instance’s IVenuePeople mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
photoUrl PhotoURL The photo’s CDN URL, if there is one.
photography IEventPhotos The instance’s IEventPhotos mixin, when available.
ping IPingFeed The instance’s IPingFeed mixin, when available.
point Geography Where it is, as WKT: its location’s point.
