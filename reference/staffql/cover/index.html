Cover

An event’s cover, inside its tickets (Event.tickets { cover }): its own for the night, or its venue’s. Its charge is also one of tickets.offers.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
buyCoatCheck
input: CoatCheckBuyInput!		 CoatCheckBuyPayload Starts paying coat check at a venue tonight through the venue’s payment provider. Asking again the same night returns the same pending or paid coat check pass. requires cover:write:self
buyCover
input: CoverBuyInput!		 CoverBuyPayload Starts paying tonight’s cover at a venue, through the venue’s provider. Stripe: finish the payment with Stripe’s payment sheet (paymentIntentClientSecret, on the venue’s account stripeAccountId, with publishableKey), then call confirmCover. Square: make a payment token with Square’s In-App Payments SDK (squareApplicationId, squareLocationId), then call payCover with it. Asking again the same night returns the same pass. requires cover:write:self
claimGuestPurchases
input: GuestPurchasesClaimInput!		 GuestPurchasesClaimPayload Attaches covers and tips bought in the App Clip before signing in to the signed-in member, by the claim tokens the clip got when it started them (POST /v1/clip/covers and /v1/clip/tips, doc/APP_CLIP_CHECKOUT.md). Each token works once, in its own audience. A cover is left unclaimed when they already hold one at that venue that night. requires cover:write:self or tipping:write:self
clearCoverNight staffql only
input: CoverNightClearInput!		 CoverNightClearPayload Puts a night back to the usual cover at a venue whose door the caller works. requires cover:write:door
confirmCover
input: CoverConfirmInput!		 CoverConfirmPayload Checks a cover payment with its provider after the app finishes paying, so the pass works before the webhook arrives. requires cover:write:self
payCover
input: CoverPayInput!		 CoverPayPayload Pays a Square venue’s cover with a payment token from Square’s In-App Payments SDK (a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), after buyCover. The pass works as soon as this returns it paid. requires cover:write:self
refundAdmission
input: AdmissionRefundInput!		 AdmissionRefundPayload Refunds a cover. Buyers can refund their own until it’s scanned at the door or the night is over; the venue’s door staff and the audience’s admins can refund any paid cover. requires cover:write:self or cover:write:door
scanAdmission staffql only
input: AdmissionScanInput!		 AdmissionScanPayload Checks a pass’s QR code at a venue’s door. The first scan of a paid pass checks them in; later ones that night are re-entry. requires cover:write:door or cover:write:venue
setCoverNight staffql only
input: CoverNightSetInput!		 CoverNightSetPayload Changes one night’s cover at a venue whose door the caller works: another price, none, or other hours. It wins over the usual cover and over an event that night. requires cover:write:door
updateVenueCover staffql only
input: VenueCoverUpdateInput!		 VenueCoverUpdatePayload Sets the usual cover for a venue whose door the caller manages. requires cover:write:door or cover:write:venue

Subscriptions

Section titled “Subscriptions”
Operation Returns Description
door staffql only
venueId: ID!		 DoorEvent! Follows a venue’s door for tonight: DoorOpened, then every cover that’s paid, scanned in or refunded, with fresh totals. For whoever may work the door: its admins, its listing managers, and a venue device with door. requires cover:write:door or cover:write:venue

Admission

Section titled “Admission”

Object · implements ICharge, Node

Someone’s cover for one night at a venue, and their pass. See CoverPass for the QR code.

Field Type Description
amount Money! The cover.
checkedInAt ISO8601DateTime When the door first scanned it.
claimCheckNumber String Coat check hanger or claim number.
createdAt ISO8601DateTime! When the record was created.
event Event The event that night, if it set the cover.
fee Money! The Hot Mess fee.
id ID! Unique identifier.
isRefundable Boolean! Whether the buyer can refund it themselves: paid, not scanned in, and the night isn’t over.
kind AdmissionKindEnum! The kind of pass (cover or coat_check).
night ISO8601Date! The local date of the cover night.
passCode String The pass QR code right now, for clients that don’t make their own. Changes every 30 seconds. The buyer only.
passSecret String The Base64 key the app makes the pass QR code with. The buyer only; null for everyone else.
profile Profile! The buyer: their name for the door and the console, their picture so the door can match faces.
quantity Int! Number of covers in this admission.
scanCount Int! How many times the door has scanned it.
status AdmissionStatus! Current payment status.
tier String The cover tier, e.g. general, vip.
total Money! What the buyer paid.
venue Venue! The venue associated with this record.

CoverCharge

Section titled “CoverCharge”

Object · implements IAdmissionOffer, ICharge

What it costs to get into a venue on a night. The apps show total, the all-in price, and take it with buyCover when payable is true; otherwise it’s paid at the door.

Field Type Description
amount Money! The cover the venue receives.
coatCheck CoatCheck The venue’s coat check service for the night, if offered.
event Event The event that night, when it sets the night’s cover.
fee Money! The Hot Mess fee in it. Part of total when the buyer pays fees; taken from the cover when the venue does. Zero when it is paid at the door.
from TimeOfDay When the cover starts, local time, e.g. 21:00.
night ISO8601Date! The night it is for. A night runs 6am to 6am, so 1am Saturday is Friday night.
override Boolean! Whether the venue changed this night’s cover from its usual one.
payable Boolean! Whether it can be paid in the app.
paymentMechanisms [String!]! Supported payment mechanisms, e.g. apple_pay, card.
price Money What it costs the buyer, all in (the lowest price for tickets). Null when the seller does not say.
seller IProvider! Who sells it: a ticketing partner, or AudienceKit itself.
tiers [CoverTier!] Cover tiers for this night, if any.
total Money! What the buyer pays, all in. The cover itself when it is paid at the door.
until TimeOfDay When the cover ends, local time, e.g. 02:00.
url String Where to buy it on the web. Null when it’s bought in the app (buyCover) or at the door.

ScanOutcome

Section titled “ScanOutcome”

Enum · staffql only

What the door should do with a scanned pass.

  • ADMIT Paid and first scan tonight: let them in.
  • COAT_CHECK_CLAIM Coat claimed / returned at coat check.
  • COAT_CHECK_IN Coat checked in at coat check.
  • EXPIRED An old code, e.g. a screenshot. Ask them to open the pass.
  • NOT_PAID The cover wasn’t paid, or was refunded or disputed.
  • RE_ENTRY Paid and already scanned in tonight: coming back in.
  • UNREADABLE Not a Hot Mess pass, or one we don’t know.
  • WRONG_VENUE A pass for another venue or another night.
