Chat

The chat module’s root lookups: the audience’s rooms, a room’s transcript and bans, and the moderators an audience can use.

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
chat IAudienceChat! The instance’s IAudienceChat mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
acknowledgeSafetyAlert staffql only
input: SafetyAlertAcknowledgeInput!		 SafetyAlertAcknowledgePayload Picks up a safety alert at your venue (“I’m on it”). The guest is told your first name is on the way; if someone else picked it up first, says who. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
chat staffql only IMutationChat The instance’s IMutationChat mixin, when available to the viewer. requires chat:moderate:*
clearChatRoom staffql only
input: ChatRoomClearInput!		 ChatRoomClearPayload Clears a venue’s or a locale’s chat room: every message in it is removed, for everyone in the room and from the transcript. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue
closeSafetyAlert staffql only
input: SafetyAlertCloseInput!		 SafetyAlertClosePayload Closes a safety alert at your venue, with how it went. The guest is told it was closed and that they can send another; never the outcome. “Something seems off” puts it in front of the audience’s team. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
connectChat staffql only
input: ChatConnectInput!		 ChatConnectPayload Moves the audience’s chat rooms to a provider (Chat::Provider), e.g. its Discord server, or changes the connection: the community, its invite link and, for providers with an API, a bot token, stored encrypted. Omitted arguments keep their value; an empty string clears it. Use disconnectChat to go back to AudienceKit’s own rooms. requires chat:write:*
disconnectChat staffql only
input: ChatDisconnectInput!		 ChatDisconnectPayload Disconnects the audience’s chat provider and forgets its bot token and channels: its rooms are AudienceKit’s own again, with whatever they held before. requires chat:write:*
moderateVenueChat staffql only
input: VenueChatModerateInput!		 VenueChatModeratePayload Kicks someone out of one venue chat, or bans or unbans them from the venue, without changing their audience access. A venue ban keeps them out of its chat and takes away their view of the venue, its events and lineups (Ability::AT_VENUE). Staff can only moderate people who were at the venue; platform admins can ban any member. The cast of a show there may KICK while it runs and for an hour after it ends (VenueShow.castModeratesUntil); banning stays with the venue. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue
pinChatMessage staffql only
input: ChatMessagePinInput!		 ChatMessagePinPayload Pins an announcement under its chat room’s title, replacing the one pinned there, or unpins it. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue
removeChatMessage staffql only
input: ChatMessageRemoveInput!		 ChatMessageRemovePayload Removes a message from its chat room. Everyone in the room stops seeing it at once; the console’s transcript keeps it, marked removed, with the reason. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue
removeMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageRemoveOwnInput!		 ChatMessageRemoveOwnPayload Removes one of your own messages from its chat room. Everyone in the room stops seeing it at once. requires chat:write:self
reportChatMessage
input: ChatMessageReportInput!		 ChatMessageReportPayload Reports a message in a venue’s or locale’s chat room to the audience’s admins, who see it in the console’s Reports queue at once and can remove it or ban the sender. Optionally blocks the sender too. A post as the venue or as a person can be reported like any other; blocking ignores it, since a listing has no sender to block. Same as reportContent with MESSAGE. requires chat:write:self
reportSafetyShiftPresence staffql only
input: SafetyShiftPresenceReportInput!		 SafetyShiftPresenceReportPayload Reports whether this device is inside a venue, for a safety shift that follows the venue’s geofence. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
seeSafetyAlert staffql only
input: SafetyAlertSeeInput!		 SafetyAlertSeePayload Records that this device opened a safety alert at its venue, so the guest knows someone saw it. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
sendChatMessage staffql only
input: ChatMessageSendInput!		 ChatMessageSendPayload Posts to a venue’s or a locale’s chat room, as the signed-in admin (STAFF), as the venue (VENUE) or as a person (HOST). Everyone in the room sees it at once. Admins post as any; venue listing managers only as their venue; talent as a person they manage, in the rooms of the venues and locales they’re featured in or host events at (Person.chat.rooms), from anywhere: text, a PHOTO or one of their EVENTs. requires chat:write:mine or chat:write:my_troupes or chat:write:venue
sendMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageSendOwnInput!		 ChatMessageSendOwnPayload Says something in a chat room as yourself. The message reaches everyone following the room at once. requires chat:write:self or chat:write:venue
setChatChannel staffql only
input: ChatChannelSetInput!		 ChatChannelSetPayload Makes a venue’s or locale’s room one of the chat provider’s channels, e.g. a Discord channel, so the apps send people there. An empty channel clears it, and the room sends people to the invite. requires chat:write:*
setSafetyShift staffql only
input: SafetyShiftSetInput!		 SafetyShiftSetPayload Turns this device’s safety alerts at a venue on or off, and sets the first name guests see when it picks one up. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
shareSafetyAlertSupport staffql only
input: SafetyAlertSupportShareInput!		 SafetyAlertSupportSharePayload Shares “Need support?” (crisis and support lines) with the guest who sent a safety alert at your venue, open or closed, with a note if you like. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
updateChatMessage staffql only
input: ChatMessageUpdateInput!		 ChatMessageUpdatePayload Edits an announcement or special: its title, its body, or when a special ends. An endsAt of now or earlier ends the special at once. requires chat:write:mine
verifyChat staffql only
input: ChatVerifyInput!		 ChatVerifyPayload Checks the audience’s stored chat bot token with its provider, without changing anything there. requires chat:write:*

BotSender

Section titled “BotSender”

Object · implements Sender

Software with a name of its own, like Glam, the AI host. Never a person, so it has no presence and nobody to block.

Field Type Description
avatarUrl PhotoURL The sender’s avatar: the bot’s art, the venue’s photo, the listing’s or the member’s. Null for the room itself.

ChatAccount

Section titled “ChatAccount”

Object

An audience’s connection to the provider that runs its chat rooms, e.g. its Discord server. Its bot token is stored encrypted and never returned.

Field Type Description
bridge Boolean! Whether an admin turned the bridge on (connectChat bridge).
bridged Boolean! Whether the bot bridges the rooms now: the bridge is on and there’s a bot token. The apps keep their rooms and the lines go both ways.
channels [ChatChannel!]! Its venues’ and locales’ channels.
externalId String! The community’s ID on the provider, e.g. a Discord server ID.
hasBotToken Boolean! Whether a bot token for the provider is stored.
id ID! Unique identifier.
inviteUrl String The link that lets people into the community.
lastError String Why the last check with the provider failed, if it did.
lastVerifiedAt ISO8601DateTime When the provider last accepted its token.
name String The community’s name on the provider.
provider ChatProvider! The provider.

ChatJoined

Section titled “ChatJoined”

Object

A chat room’s first event, on joining it: who is there, its recent history and its pinned announcement.

Field Type Description
expiresBefore ISO8601DateTime! When the room stops showing messages: those sent before it have expired (its chat retention) for everyone but its admins, whose history goes back further. The apps put a divider there between what has cleared and what is current.
history [ChatMessagePosted!]! The room’s last 100 messages, oldest first: the last 24 hours of it, or all of it for its admins. Removed messages and people you blocked are left out.
outOfRange Boolean! Whether you’re in the room only because you’re an admin, away from it. ChatRangeChanged says when that changes.
pinnedMessage ChatMessagePosted The announcement pinned under the room’s title, however old.
roster ChatRoster! Who is in the room now, and who’s your friend.

ChatLineupPresence

Section titled “ChatLineupPresence”

Object

Someone on what’s on in a chat room now (an event’s people, tonight’s lineup at the venue), and how reachable whoever hosts as them is (see LineupPresence). A listing is public, so everyone in the room sees it.

Field Type Description
personId ID! The person (listing) on the lineup.
presence Presence! ONLINE while anyone hosting as them is in a chat room, PUSH while a notification reaches one of them, else OFFLINE (also for someone nobody has hosted as tonight).

ChatMessagePosted

Section titled “ChatMessagePosted”

Object

A message in a chat room, as it was posted.

Field Type Description

senderPresence Presence ONLINE when its sender is in the room now; PUSH when they aren’t but a notification reaches them, which only their friends and the room’s admins see; otherwise null, as for a post as the venue or a listing.

Field Type Description

ChatKind

Section titled “ChatKind”

Enum

What a chat message is. Only messages with a role can be anything but TEXT or REQUEST.

  • ANNOUNCEMENT A title, the message as its body and an optional photo. One per room can be pinned under its title.
  • EVENT One of the audience’s events, with the message as a caption.
  • PHOTO A photo, with the message as a caption.
  • PROMPT One of the event’s chat prompts (prompt), posted as the audience. The newest PINNED one shows under the room’s title.
  • REQUEST A song request or karaoke signup (request), with the message as a note to the host. Anyone in a venue room may send one while the venue takes them (requestSong).
  • SPECIAL A title and body that stop showing at endsAt.
  • TEXT Just what they wrote.
