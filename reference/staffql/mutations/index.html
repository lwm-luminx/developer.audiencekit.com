Mutations

Every mutation takes one input argument and returns a payload. The permissions listed are the ones that allow it, any one of them (see Authentication). When a record fails validation, the error’s extensions carry the field errors (see Errors).

recordCardViews

remindSafetyAlert

removeAppReviewCode staffql only

input: ContentReportInput!		 ContentReportPayload Reports a message, person, venue, ping, photo, post or comment to the audience’s admins, who see it in the console’s Reports queue at once. Optionally blocks the person responsible, when there is one: a message’s sender (not for posts as the venue or a person), the person themself, a ping’s sender, a comment’s author. requires users:write:self
reportLocation
input: LocationReportInput!		 LocationReportPayload Records the user’s position and returns the venue they’re in, or what’s near them. requires users:write:self
reportVenueOpen
input: VenueOpenReportInput!		 VenueOpenReportPayload Says a venue reported closed is still open. A reviewer decides; the venue is back in tonight’s views sooner when enough people (or its staff) say so. requires users:write:self
resetAppReview staffql only
input: AppReviewResetInput!		 AppReviewResetPayload Does what rake app_review:reset does: resets the sandbox reviewer account, refreshes the demo venue and its sample nights, sets the review code and turns App Review on for Hot Mess and AudienceKit Demo (AppReviewState). It also resets the second reviewer, banned from one Hot Mess venue, with its own code (AppReviewCode). Platform administrators only. requires platform:admin:*
resetAudienceInvite staffql only
input: AudienceInviteResetInput!		 AudienceInviteResetPayload Makes a new invite link for the audience. The old link stops working; people who already joined keep their listings. requires audience:admin:*
resolveContentReport staffql only
input: ContentReportResolveInput!		 ContentReportResolvePayload Resolves a report in the audience’s Reports queue, and the other open reports on the same thing. removeContent takes the content down first (removes the message, hides the photo, ends the ping); banUser bans the person responsible from the audience, as banAudienceMember does. requires audience:moderate:*
respondToFriendRequest
input: FriendRequestRespondInput!		 FriendRequestRespondPayload Answers a friend request someone sent the viewer. Accepting makes them friends and tells the sender; “Not now” hides it and tells nobody. requires users:write:self
reviewSafetyAlert staffql only
input: SafetyAlertReviewInput!		 SafetyAlertReviewPayload Records what the audience’s team decided about a flagged safety alert, which takes it out of the needs-a-look list. It never bans anyone; banAudienceMember does that. requires audience:moderate:*
revokeServiceToken staffql only
input: ServiceTokenRevokeInput!		 ServiceTokenRevokePayload Revokes a service token. It stops signing in at once; a JWT it already traded for lasts at most an hour. requires audience:admin:*
savePromoPlacement staffql only
input: PromoPlacementSaveInput!		 PromoPlacementSavePayload Books a promoted, charity or announcement card into the apps’ layouts, or changes one when id is given. Where it shows is contextual only: screens, locales, nights and venue or event tags. requires audience:write:*
sendFriendRequest
input: FriendRequestSendInput!		 FriendRequestSendPayload Asks someone contact matching found to be friends, and pushes them. Asking someone who already asked the viewer accepts their request. requires users:write:self
sendSafetyAlert
input: SafetyAlertSendInput!		 SafetyAlertSendPayload Tells the staff on shift at the venue you’re at that you feel unsafe (only while the app places you there). Where you are and what is going on are optional; send it first and add them after (updateSafetyAlert). While your alert there is open, sending again adds to it. requires users:write:self
sendSafetyAlertMessage
input: SafetyAlertMessageSendInput!		 SafetyAlertMessageSendPayload Sends a message in a safety alert’s private thread: from the guest who sent it while it is open, or from staff at its venue. Never shown in venue chat. requires users:write:self or chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
setAiCapability staffql only

setAudienceAdmin staffql only

setTribes
input: TribeSetInput!		 TribeSetPayload Replaces the signed-in user’s tribes here with these, for the picker’s Done. Tribes they keep keep their privacy; new ones are private. Refused, changing nothing, past audience.tribeCap tribes. requires users:write:self
startAiProviderSignIn staffql only

startContactPhoneVerification

submitContribution
input: ContributionSubmitInput!		 ContributionSubmitPayload Suggests a missing venue or event, a correction, a closure, a tag or a performer for the audience’s listings. unbanAudienceMember staffql only
input: AudienceMemberUnbanInput!		 AudienceMemberUnbanPayload Lets a banned member sign in and chat again. requires audience:moderate:*
unblockUser

unlinkAiProvider staffql only

updateArbiterPrompt staffql only

updatePromoCardCopy staffql only

updateSafetyAlert

input: SafetyAlertCloseInput!		 SafetyAlertClosePayload Closes a safety alert at your venue, with how it went. pinChatMessage staffql only

removeMyChatMessage

reportChatMessage

sendMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageSendOwnInput!		 ChatMessageSendOwnPayload Says something in a chat room as yourself. The message reaches everyone following the room at once. requires chat:write:self or chat:write:venue
setChatChannel staffql only
input: ChatChannelSetInput!		 ChatChannelSetPayload Makes a venue’s or locale’s room one of the chat provider’s channels, e.g. a Discord channel, so the apps send people there. An empty channel clears it, and the room sends people to the invite. requires chat:write:*
setSafetyShift staffql only
input: SafetyShiftSetInput!		 SafetyShiftSetPayload Turns this device’s safety alerts at a venue on or off, and sets the first name guests see when it picks one up. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
shareSafetyAlertSupport staffql only
input: SafetyAlertSupportShareInput!		 SafetyAlertSupportSharePayload Shares “Need support?” (crisis and support lines) with the guest who sent a safety alert at your venue, open or closed, with a note if you like. requires chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
updateChatMessage staffql only
input: ChatMessageUpdateInput!		 ChatMessageUpdatePayload Edits an announcement or special: its title, its body, or when a special ends. An endsAt of now or earlier ends the special at once. requires chat:write:mine
verifyChat staffql only
input: ChatVerifyInput!		 ChatVerifyPayload Checks the audience’s stored chat bot token with its provider, without changing anything there. requires chat:write:*

clearCoverNight staffql only

confirmCover

payCover

refundAdmission
input: AdmissionRefundInput!		 AdmissionRefundPayload Refunds a cover. Buyers can refund their own until it’s scanned at the door or the night is over; the venue’s door staff and the audience’s admins can refund any paid cover. requires cover:write:self or cover:write:door
scanAdmission staffql only
input: AdmissionScanInput!		 AdmissionScanPayload Checks a pass’s QR code at a venue’s door. The first scan of a paid pass checks them in; later ones that night are re-entry. requires cover:write:door or cover:write:venue
setCoverNight staffql only
input: CoverNightSetInput!		 CoverNightSetPayload Changes one night’s cover at a venue whose door the caller works: another price, none, or other hours. It wins over the usual cover and over an event that night. requires cover:write:door
updateVenueCover staffql only
input: VenueCoverUpdateInput!		 VenueCoverUpdatePayload Sets the usual cover for a venue whose door the caller manages. requires cover:write:door or cover:write:venue
