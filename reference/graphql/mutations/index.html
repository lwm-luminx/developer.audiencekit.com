Mutations

Every mutation takes one input argument and returns a payload. The permissions listed are the ones that allow it, any one of them (see Authentication). When a record fails validation, the error’s extensions carry the field errors (see Errors).

Core

Section titled “Core”

See Core for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
acceptTerms
input: TermsAcceptInput!		 TermsAcceptPayload Records that the signed-in user agreed to the terms of use, which forbid objectionable content and abuse. The apps ask before anyone chats. requires users:write:self
blockUser
input: BlockedUserAddInput!		 BlockedUserAddPayload Blocks someone: neither sees the other’s chat messages, presence, pings or whereabouts any more. They aren’t told. The audience’s admins are, in the console’s Reports queue. requires users:write:self
cancelSafetyAlert
input: SafetyAlertCancelInput!		 SafetyAlertCancelPayload Cancels your open safety alert: you’re OK now. Staff on shift are told. requires users:write:self
checkContacts
input: ContactsCheckInput!		 ContactsCheckPayload Checks the hashed contacts from the viewer’s phone (ContactMatching: SHA-256 hex of “first|last|+E164”, one per number; contacts without a first and last name left out) and turns matching on. Replaces their last check. Once a day. requires users:write:self
createCalendarFeed
input: CalendarFeedCreateInput!		 CalendarFeedCreatePayload The signed-in user’s calendar subscription link in this audience (doc/CALENDAR_SYNC.md), made on first ask. reset gives it a new secret, so the old link stops working. requires users:write:self
deleteAccount
input: AccountDeleteInput!		 AccountDeletePayload Deletes the signed-in user’s account and everything recorded about them (see UserDataEraser), and signs them out everywhere. It can’t be undone. requires users:write:self
deleteCalendarFeed
input: CalendarFeedDeleteInput!		 CalendarFeedDeletePayload Ends the signed-in user’s calendar subscription link in this audience: calendar apps subscribed to it stop getting updates. requires users:write:self
disableContactMatching
input: ContactMatchingDisableInput!		 ContactMatchingDisablePayload Turns contact matching off: deletes their number, match name and every code, at once, and withdraws requests they sent that nobody answered. Friends they made stay. requires users:write:self
identifyWithTribe
input: TribeIdentifyInput!		 TribeIdentifyPayload Identifies the signed-in user with one of the audience’s tribes, or changes whether that’s private. Private is the default: only people in the same tribe see it, and it is always private unless audience.publicTribes is on. Refused past audience.tribeCap tribes. requires users:write:self
leaveTribe
input: TribeLeaveInput!		 TribeLeavePayload Removes the signed-in user from one of the tribes they identify with. Nothing is kept. requires users:write:self
recordCardViews
input: CardViewsRecordInput!		 CardViewsRecordPayload Records that promoted cards were on screen. Send only the cards actually shown. requires users:write:self
registerDevice
input: DeviceRegisterInput!		 DeviceRegisterPayload Stores the push notification token for the device this session signed in on. requires users:write:self
remindSafetyAlert
input: SafetyAlertRemindInput!		 SafetyAlertRemindPayload Sends your open safety alert again: staff on shift hear it again, and the venue’s managers too. At most every 30 seconds. requires users:write:self
reportContent
input: ContentReportInput!		 ContentReportPayload Reports a message, person, venue, ping, photo, post or comment to the audience’s admins, who see it in the console’s Reports queue at once. Optionally blocks the person responsible, when there is one: a message’s sender (not for posts as the venue or a person), the person themself, a ping’s sender, a comment’s author. requires users:write:self
reportLocation
input: LocationReportInput!		 LocationReportPayload Records the user’s position and returns the venue they’re in, or what’s near them. requires users:write:self
reportVenueOpen
input: VenueOpenReportInput!		 VenueOpenReportPayload Says a venue reported closed is still open. A reviewer decides; the venue is back in tonight’s views sooner when enough people (or its staff) say so. requires users:write:self
respondToFriendRequest
input: FriendRequestRespondInput!		 FriendRequestRespondPayload Answers a friend request someone sent the viewer. Accepting makes them friends and tells the sender; “Not now” hides it and tells nobody. requires users:write:self
sendFriendRequest
input: FriendRequestSendInput!		 FriendRequestSendPayload Asks someone contact matching found to be friends, and pushes them. Asking someone who already asked the viewer accepts their request. requires users:write:self
sendSafetyAlert
input: SafetyAlertSendInput!		 SafetyAlertSendPayload Tells the staff on shift at the venue you’re at that you feel unsafe (only while the app places you there). Where you are and what is going on are optional; send it first and add them after (updateSafetyAlert). While your alert there is open, sending again adds to it. requires users:write:self
sendSafetyAlertMessage
input: SafetyAlertMessageSendInput!		 SafetyAlertMessageSendPayload Sends a message in a safety alert’s private thread: from the guest who sent it while it is open, or from staff at its venue. Never shown in venue chat. requires users:write:self or chat:moderate:mine or chat:moderate:venue or audience:moderate:*
setChatInitials
input: ChatInitialsSetInput!		 ChatInitialsSetPayload Shows the signed-in user’s initials in chat rooms instead of their photo, or their photo again. requires users:write:self
setContactMatchName
input: ContactMatchNameSetInput!		 ContactMatchNameSetPayload Changes the first and last name friends match the viewer on. Their own codes follow at once. requires users:write:self
setRecentChatPush
input: RecentChatPushSetInput!		 RecentChatPushSetPayload Turns on or off pushes about new messages in chat rooms the signed-in user left in the last 30 minutes (at most one every 10 minutes per room). Off until they turn it on. requires users:write:self
setTribes
input: TribeSetInput!		 TribeSetPayload Replaces the signed-in user’s tribes here with these, for the picker’s Done. Tribes they keep keep their privacy; new ones are private. Refused, changing nothing, past audience.tribeCap tribes. requires users:write:self
startContactPhoneVerification
input: ContactPhoneVerificationStartInput!		 ContactPhoneVerificationStartPayload Saves the name friends will match the viewer on and texts a six-digit code to their mobile number (verifyContactPhone). Up to five codes a day, 30 seconds apart. requires users:write:self
submitContribution
input: ContributionSubmitInput!		 ContributionSubmitPayload Suggests a missing venue or event, a correction, a closure, a tag or a performer for the audience’s listings. A reviewer decides; nothing changes until they approve. Local rules are corrected with submitLocalRulesCorrection, by venue staff. requires users:write:self
unblockUser
input: BlockedUserRemoveInput!		 BlockedUserRemovePayload Unblocks someone the signed-in user blocked. requires users:write:self
updateSafetyAlert
input: SafetyAlertUpdateInput!		 SafetyAlertUpdatePayload Adds where you are and what is going on to your open safety alert. Concerns add to the ones you picked; left-out arguments keep what you said. requires users:write:self
verifyContactPhone
input: ContactPhoneVerifyInput!		 ContactPhoneVerifyPayload Checks the code startContactPhoneVerification texted. Right, the number is theirs: it moves off any other account that had it. Five tries per code. requires users:write:self

Chat

Section titled “Chat”

See Chat for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
removeMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageRemoveOwnInput!		 ChatMessageRemoveOwnPayload Removes one of your own messages from its chat room. Everyone in the room stops seeing it at once. requires chat:write:self
reportChatMessage
input: ChatMessageReportInput!		 ChatMessageReportPayload Reports a message in a venue’s or locale’s chat room to the audience’s admins, who see it in the console’s Reports queue at once and can remove it or ban the sender. Optionally blocks the sender too. A post as the venue or as a person can be reported like any other; blocking ignores it, since a listing has no sender to block. Same as reportContent with MESSAGE. requires chat:write:self
sendMyChatMessage
input: ChatMessageSendOwnInput!		 ChatMessageSendOwnPayload Says something in a chat room as yourself. The message reaches everyone following the room at once. requires chat:write:self or chat:write:venue

Cover

Section titled “Cover”

See Cover for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
buyCoatCheck
input: CoatCheckBuyInput!		 CoatCheckBuyPayload Starts paying coat check at a venue tonight through the venue’s payment provider. Asking again the same night returns the same pending or paid coat check pass. requires cover:write:self
buyCover
input: CoverBuyInput!		 CoverBuyPayload Starts paying tonight’s cover at a venue, through the venue’s provider. Stripe: finish the payment with Stripe’s payment sheet (paymentIntentClientSecret, on the venue’s account stripeAccountId, with publishableKey), then call confirmCover. Square: make a payment token with Square’s In-App Payments SDK (squareApplicationId, squareLocationId), then call payCover with it. Asking again the same night returns the same pass. requires cover:write:self
claimGuestPurchases
input: GuestPurchasesClaimInput!		 GuestPurchasesClaimPayload Attaches covers and tips bought in the App Clip before signing in to the signed-in member, by the claim tokens the clip got when it started them (POST /v1/clip/covers and /v1/clip/tips, doc/APP_CLIP_CHECKOUT.md). Each token works once, in its own audience. A cover is left unclaimed when they already hold one at that venue that night. requires cover:write:self or tipping:write:self
confirmCover
input: CoverConfirmInput!		 CoverConfirmPayload Checks a cover payment with its provider after the app finishes paying, so the pass works before the webhook arrives. requires cover:write:self
payCover
input: CoverPayInput!		 CoverPayPayload Pays a Square venue’s cover with a payment token from Square’s In-App Payments SDK (a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), after buyCover. The pass works as soon as this returns it paid. requires cover:write:self
refundAdmission
input: AdmissionRefundInput!		 AdmissionRefundPayload Refunds a cover. Buyers can refund their own until it’s scanned at the door or the night is over; the venue’s door staff and the audience’s admins can refund any paid cover. requires cover:write:self or cover:write:door

Pair up

Section titled “Pair up”

See Pair up for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
acceptPairUp
input: PairUpAcceptInput!		 PairUpAcceptPayload Accepts your pairing’s topic. Once both of you accept, each sees who to look for. requires pair_up:write:self
cancelPairUp
input: PairUpCancelInput!		 PairUpCancelPayload Cancels a pairing at any point: neither of you sees the other from then on. Cancelling your pairing takes you out of Quickie for tonight (your partner looks for someone new); a bonus topic just ends. requires pair_up:write:self
joinPairUp
input: PairUpJoinInput!		 PairUpJoinPayload Opts in to Quickie at a venue for tonight (only while the app places you there), or changes the hint your partner sees. After a meeting, looks for someone new. requires pair_up:write:self
leavePairUp
input: PairUpLeaveInput!		 PairUpLeavePayload Leaves Quickie at a venue for tonight, cancelling your pairing if you have one. requires pair_up:write:self
scanPairUpCode
input: PairUpCodeScanInput!		 PairUpCodeScanPayload Scans a Quickie code. Your partner’s confirms you met (both see You met!); someone else’s gives the two of you a fresh topic instead. Anything else says why, without changing anything. requires pair_up:write:self
sharePairUp
input: PairUpShareInput!		 PairUpSharePayload Agrees to share your meeting in the venue’s chat. Once both of you have, the venue posts your names (first name and last initial) and your topic. requires pair_up:write:self
showPairUpCode
input: PairUpCodeShowInput!		 PairUpCodeShowPayload A fresh QR code to show your partner once you both accept. It works once, only for them, and replaces the one you showed before. requires pair_up:write:self
skipPairUp
input: PairUpSkipInput!		 PairUpSkipPayload Skips the topic for a new one (both accept again), or the partner: both look for someone new and aren’t paired together again tonight. requires pair_up:write:self

People

Section titled “People”

See People for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
setReadiness
input: ReadinessSetInput!		 ReadinessSetPayload Says who is ready for a curtain call: the venue, a performer or someone on the support staff. The show runs on GO (advanceLineup waits for it unless forced). requires people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue or users:write:self
updateLineupTask
input: LineupTaskUpdateInput!		 LineupTaskUpdatePayload Ticks, unticks, removes, puts back or rewords a lineup entry’s task. Fields left out aren’t changed. Anyone working the show ticks; whoever may change the lineup removes, puts back and rewords (only an entry’s own task has words of its own). requires people:write:mine or people:write:my_troupes or events:write:venue or users:write:self

Photography

Section titled “Photography”

See Photography for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
reportEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoReportInput!		 EventPhotoReportPayload Reports a photographer’s photo, for example a photo of yourself you want taken down. It’s hidden at once, until the audience’s admins look at it in the console’s Reports queue; the photographer sees it was reported. reportContent with PHOTO does the same. requires photography:write:self

input: PingEndInput!		 PingEndPayload Ends the signed-in user’s ping. requires ping:write:self
joinPing

leavePing

sendPing

Posts

Section titled “Posts”

See Posts for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
addEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoTakeInput!		 EventPhotoTakePayload Adds a photo taken with the app’s camera at a venue (only while the app places you there): it’s marked AMATEUR, credited to you, put on the event under way there, and tagged with whoever is on stage, so it shows on the venue’s, the event’s and their pages (IPosts.photos). requires posts:write:self
createPostComment
input: PostCommentCreateInput!		 PostCommentCreatePayload Comments on a venue’s or a person’s post, as the signed-in user. Someone banned from the venue’s chat can’t, and nobody can once its comments are closed. requires posts:write:self
mutePostAuthor
input: PostAuthorMuteInput!		 PostAuthorMutePayload Hides a venue’s or a person’s posts from the signed-in user, or shows them again. Name exactly one of venueId and personId. requires posts:write:self
removeEventPhoto
input: EventPhotoRemoveInput!		 EventPhotoRemovePayload Removes a photo you took with the app’s camera (addEventPhoto), from every page it was on. requires posts:write:self
removePostComment
input: PostCommentRemoveInput!		 PostCommentRemovePayload Removes a comment on a post: its author’s own, or any on a post for whoever manages its poster (the venue’s managers, the talent listing’s) and admins. requires posts:write:self or posts:moderate:mine

Requests

Section titled “Requests”

See Requests for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
requestSong
input: SongRequestCreateInput!		 SongRequestCreatePayload Asks for a song, or signs up to sing one, in a venue’s chat room. Everyone in the room sees the REQUEST line, and the host finds it in the queue. Refused while the venue is not taking requests of that kind (see Venue.requests for why). requires requests:write:self
withdrawSongRequest
input: SongRequestWithdrawInput!		 SongRequestWithdrawPayload Takes back a song request or karaoke signup of yours that is still waiting. Its chat line shows it withdrawn. requires requests:write:self

Rsvp

Section titled “Rsvp”

See Rsvp for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
cancelRsvp
input: RSVPCancelInput!		 RSVPCancelPayload Takes back the user’s RSVP to an event, as if they’d never answered (UNSURE). Someone holding a ticket to it is still going: refund the ticket to stop going. requires rsvp:write:self
rsvpEvent
input: EventRSVPInput!		 EventRSVPPayload Sets the user’s RSVP to an event in AudienceKit. It isn’t sent to Facebook, which no longer lets apps RSVP for people. A ticket holder stays going whatever they answer. requires rsvp:write:self

Tipping

Section titled “Tipping”

See Tipping for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
confirmTip
input: TipConfirmInput!		 TipConfirmPayload Checks a tip payment with its provider after the app finishes paying, before the webhook arrives. requires tipping:write:self
payTip
input: TipPayInput!		 TipPayPayload Pays a tip to a performer who takes Square with a payment token from Square’s In-App Payments SDK (a card, Apple Pay or Google Pay), after startTip. requires tipping:write:self
startTip
input: TipStartInput!		 TipStartPayload Starts tipping a performer in the app, through their own Stripe or Square account (the IN_APP option of their tipping mixin). Stripe: finish with Stripe’s payment sheet (paymentIntentClientSecret, on their account stripeAccountId, with publishableKey), then call confirmTip. Square: make a payment token with Square’s In-App Payments SDK (squareApplicationId, squareLocationId), then call payTip with it. Asking again for the same amount soon after returns the same tip. requires tipping:write:self

Trivia

Section titled “Trivia”

See Trivia for the inputs and payloads.

Operation Returns Description
voteTrivia
input: TriviaVoteCastInput!		 TriviaVoteCastPayload Votes in a trivia round: someone at the venue picks a choice (changing it is fine while voting is open). Only counts are shown; nobody is named or scored. requires trivia:write:self
