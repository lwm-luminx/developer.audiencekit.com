Venues

An audience’s venues, a lookup of one by its UUID, and a venue’s staff devices.

Root fields

Section titled “Root fields”
Field Type Description
venues IAudienceVenues! The instance’s IAudienceVenues mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.

Objects

Section titled “Objects”

ApproximateCount

Section titled “ApproximateCount”

Object

A count rounded down into a bucket (none, a few, 5+, 10+, 25+, 50+, 100+), never exact.

Field Type Description
atLeast Int! The bucket’s floor: 0, 1, 5, 10, 25, 50 or 100.
label String! How to show it, e.g. “A few” or “10+”.

Venue

Section titled “Venue”

Object · implements HasBanner, HasPhoto, HasTags, HasTaskTemplates, HasTimestamps, Node

A venue in the audience.

Field Type Description
activity VenueActivity How busy it is right now, approximately. Admins and its managers only. requires venues:read:mine or venues:read:venue
adultVenue Boolean! Whether it is flagged for adult content: its posters and TV marquee may use the adult-venue slogan lines.
archived Boolean! Whether it closed for good: kept for its history, listed nowhere.
bannerPhotos [BannerPhoto!]! Its banner images, in the order the apps rotate through.
bannerUrl PhotoURL Its Google place photo when available, else its first banner image, own photo or Facebook Page’s cover photo.
canSendSafetyAlert Boolean! Whether the viewer can send a safety alert here now (sendSafetyAlert): the app places them at the venue. The apps show “Get help” only then.
chat IVenueChat! The instance’s IVenueChat mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
chatRetentionMinutes Int! How many minutes its chat room shows a message before it expires: its own window, else the default.
chatRetentionOverridden Boolean! Whether the venue set its own chat retention window.
createdAt ISO8601DateTime! When the record was created.
description String Its public description.
distance Float Metres from the position the venues were looked up near.
distanceTolerance Int! Distance in metres counted as being at the venue.
events IVenueEvents! The instance’s IVenueEvents mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
facets [String!]! The keys of the facets switched on for it, e.g. karaoke, in key order: each is a tag the system keeps (Tag.facet) and turns on the edges of the mixins declaring it (@mixin(tag:)). Only those whose module is installed.
friends IVenueFriends! The instance’s IVenueFriends mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
hidden Boolean! Whether the venue is hidden.
id ID! Its UUID.
karaoke IVenueKaraoke The instance’s IVenueKaraoke mixin, when available. Null unless it has the karaoke facet (its facets).
listed Boolean! False for a community space (its Community space tag), like a pride center that hosts events: listed after the venues to go out to and left out of nearby (doc/ORGANIZERS.md).
localRules VenueLocalRules Its own rules (age policy, licence, last call), as approved; null when none were.
locale Locale! The city or area containing this venue.
location Location Its geographic location.
marqueeSlogans Boolean! Whether its TV marquee (Velvet, the web sign at hotmess.social/venues/<id>/display) shows Hot Mess slogan slides between the lineup: on unless the venue turns them off.
music IVenueMusic! The instance’s IVenueMusic mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
name String The venue’s name, or its Facebook page’s.
order Int! Its display order.
ordering IVenueOrdering The instance’s IVenueOrdering mixin, when available. Null unless it has the ordering facet (its facets).
pairUp IVenuePairUp The instance’s IVenuePairUp mixin, when available. Null unless it has the pair_up facet (its facets).
pairUpEnabled Boolean! Whether Quickie is on there (Venue.pairUp): on unless the venue opted out.
people IVenuePeople! The instance’s IVenuePeople mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
photoUrl PhotoURL The photo’s CDN URL, if there is one.
photography IEventPhotos The instance’s IEventPhotos mixin, when available.
ping IPingFeed The instance’s IPingFeed mixin, when available.
point Geography Where it is, as WKT: its location’s point.
posts IPosts! The instance’s IPosts mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
profile IListingProfile! The instance’s IListingProfile mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
reportedClosed Boolean! Whether it was reported closed and that isn’t decided yet: left out of tonight’s views, its page kept.
requests IVenueRequests! The instance’s IVenueRequests mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
safetyStaffOnShift Boolean! Whether anyone on shift here hears safety alerts right now; the app offers other ways to get help when not.
tagLayers
includeHidden: Boolean		 VenueTagLayers! Its tags in layers: its own, its regular nights and its upcoming events’ one-off tags (doc/design-system/TribesAndTags.md). For a venue screen, not lists.
tags [Tag!]! Its audience tags, including tags connected to module facets, by name.
taskTemplates [TaskTemplate!]! Its lineup task templates, in order. Empty for anyone but whoever keeps them (its managers; an event’s hosts too).
tickets IVenueTickets! The instance’s IVenueTickets mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
trivia IVenueTrivia! The instance’s IVenueTrivia mixin. Left out of the schema where its module isn’t installed.
updatedAt ISO8601DateTime! When the record was last updated.
visibility ListingVisibility! Whether full listing details are public on the web.

VenueLocalRules

Section titled “VenueLocalRules”

Object

A venue’s own rules, as its staff corrected them and a reviewer approved. The jurisdiction’s public rules (Locale.liquor) outrank them where they disagree.

Field Type Description
agePolicy String all_ages, 18_plus, 21_plus or mixed.
closingTime String When it closes, HH:MM local time.
lastCall String Its last call, HH:MM local time.
licenseClass String Its liquor licence class.
notes String Anything else about its rules.
reentry String allowed, not_allowed or fee.
verifiedAt ISO8601DateTime When it was last approved.

VenueOneOffTags

Section titled “VenueOneOffTags”

Object

An upcoming event’s tags that aren’t among its venue’s own or regular-night tags. They stay on the event and never describe the venue.

Field Type Description
event Event! The event.
tags [Tag!]! Its one-off tags, by name.

VenueRegularNight

Section titled “VenueRegularNight”

Object

One of a venue’s regular nights: a tag its events or series carried in enough of the last 13 weeks. Worked out daily; staff can hide one but not edit it.

Field Type Description
fadesOn ISO8601Date! When it drops off the venue if it doesn’t run again.
hidden Boolean! Whether its staff hid it. Only managers ever see hidden ones.
lastRunOn ISO8601Date! When it last ran there.
series EventSeries The series most of its nights came from, if any.
status VenueRegularNightStatus! How it is going.
tag Tag! The tag.
weeks [Boolean!]! Whether it ran in each of the last 13 weeks, oldest first; the last is the week ending today.
weeksRun Int! In how many of the last 13 weeks it ran.

VenueTagLayers

Section titled “VenueTagLayers”

Object

A venue’s tags in three layers (VenueTagLayers): its own, its regular nights and its upcoming events’ one-off tags. Matching weighs them in that order. They only highlight: apps never sort or hide anything by them.

Field Type Description
knownFor [Tag!]! Its own tags, set by its staff or the audience’s admins.
oneOff [VenueOneOffTags!]! Its next upcoming events that carry tags outside the other two layers, soonest first.
regularNights [VenueRegularNight!]! Its regular nights, most often first.
viewerMatchScore Int! How strongly the signed-in user’s own tribes match: 3 for each matching tag it is known for, 2 for each regular night and 1 for each one-off tag. 0 when signed out.
viewerMatches [Tag!]! The tags in any layer that the signed-in user identifies with as a tribe, for a “You sing karaoke” badge. Only ever their own; empty when signed out.

Enums

Section titled “Enums”

VenueRegularNightStatus

Section titled “VenueRegularNightStatus”

Enum

How a regular night is going, from the last 13 weeks of the venue’s events.

  • FADING Hasn’t run for 6 weeks; it drops off at fadesOn.
  • MONTHLY Runs about once a month, or from a monthly series.
  • OCCASIONAL Runs now and then.
  • STEADY Ran in at least 8 of the last 13 weeks.
