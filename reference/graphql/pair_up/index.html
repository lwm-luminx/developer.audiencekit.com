Pair up

Quickie at a venue: pairs two people there to talk about a shared topic. A facet projected from Venue.pairUpEnabled: every venue has it unless it opted out.

Mutations

Section titled “Mutations”
Operation Returns Description
acceptPairUp
input: PairUpAcceptInput!		 PairUpAcceptPayload Accepts your pairing’s topic. Once both of you accept, each sees who to look for. requires pair_up:write:self
cancelPairUp
input: PairUpCancelInput!		 PairUpCancelPayload Cancels a pairing at any point: neither of you sees the other from then on. Cancelling your pairing takes you out of Quickie for tonight (your partner looks for someone new); a bonus topic just ends. requires pair_up:write:self
joinPairUp
input: PairUpJoinInput!		 PairUpJoinPayload Opts in to Quickie at a venue for tonight (only while the app places you there), or changes the hint your partner sees. After a meeting, looks for someone new. requires pair_up:write:self
leavePairUp
input: PairUpLeaveInput!		 PairUpLeavePayload Leaves Quickie at a venue for tonight, cancelling your pairing if you have one. requires pair_up:write:self
scanPairUpCode
input: PairUpCodeScanInput!		 PairUpCodeScanPayload Scans a Quickie code. Your partner’s confirms you met (both see You met!); someone else’s gives the two of you a fresh topic instead. Anything else says why, without changing anything. requires pair_up:write:self
sharePairUp
input: PairUpShareInput!		 PairUpSharePayload Agrees to share your meeting in the venue’s chat. Once both of you have, the venue posts your names (first name and last initial) and your topic. requires pair_up:write:self
showPairUpCode
input: PairUpCodeShowInput!		 PairUpCodeShowPayload A fresh QR code to show your partner once you both accept. It works once, only for them, and replaces the one you showed before. requires pair_up:write:self
skipPairUp
input: PairUpSkipInput!		 PairUpSkipPayload Skips the topic for a new one (both accept again), or the partner: both look for someone new and aren’t paired together again tonight. requires pair_up:write:self

Objects

Section titled “Objects”

PairUpMatch

Section titled “PairUpMatch”

Object

A Quickie pairing as one of its two people sees it.

Field Type Description
accepted Boolean! Whether the viewer accepted the topic.
bonus Boolean! A fresh topic from scanning someone else’s code: already accepted, no code needed.
endReason PairUpEndReason Why it ended; null while it lasts.
endedAt ISO8601DateTime When it ended.
expiresAt ISO8601DateTime! When it expires with the night (or the venue’s chat window).
id ID! Its ID.
metAt ISO8601DateTime When one scanned the other’s code.
partner PairUpPartner Who to look for, once both accepted and while it lasts. Null before, and as soon as either cancels.
partnerAccepted Boolean! Whether their partner accepted the topic.
partnerShared Boolean! Whether their partner agreed to share the meeting in the chat.
shared Boolean! Whether the viewer agreed to share the meeting in the venue’s chat (sharePairUp). It’s posted once both have.
topic PairUpTopic! What to talk about.

PairUpPartner

Section titled “PairUpPartner”

Object

Just enough to find a Quickie partner in the room, shown once both accept. Never where they are.

Field Type Description
avatarUrl PhotoURL Their picture, tiny (96 pixels square), and only while the pairing lasts. Null when they have none.
id ID! Their user id, to block or report them (blockUser).
lookFor String The hint they wrote, e.g. “red jacket by the bar”.
name String! Their name: in full for friends, first name and last initial for anyone else.

PairUpState

Section titled “PairUpState”

Object

The viewer’s Quickie at a venue tonight.

Field Type Description
bonus PairUpMatch A fresh topic shared with someone whose code they scanned (or who scanned theirs) by mistake, while it lasts.
expiresAt ISO8601DateTime! When their opt-in expires.
lookFor String The hint they wrote for their partner.
match PairUpMatch Their pairing: the current one, or while SEARCHING the last one when it ended. Null before the first.
metCount Int! How many conversations they had tonight: meetings, counted again if they meet the same person twice. The night’s badge.
status PairUpStatus! Where they are.

PairUpTopic

Section titled “PairUpTopic”

Object

Something for a pair to talk about, from the hand-written list (config/pair_up_topics.yml).

Field Type Description
key String! Its stable key.
text String! The topic, as both of them see it.

Enums

Section titled “Enums”

PairUpEndReason

Section titled “PairUpEndReason”

Enum

Why a pairing ended.

  • BLOCKED A block now stands between them.
  • LEFT One of them cancelled or left Quickie.
  • SKIPPED One of them asked for a new partner.

PairUpScanOutcome

Section titled “PairUpScanOutcome”

Enum

What scanning a Quickie code did. Only MET and DIFFERENT change anything; none is the scanner’s fault, so the apps word them gently.

  • DIFFERENT Someone else’s code. The two of them get a fresh topic instead: match (already accepted, no code needed).
  • ENDED The pairing that code was for is over.
  • MET Their partner’s code: they met. match is the met pairing.
  • OWN Their own code.
  • UNREADABLE Not a Quickie code this account can use.
  • USED Their partner’s code, but not the newest one: scan it again.

PairUpSkip

Section titled “PairUpSkip”

Enum

What skipPairUp skips.

  • PARTNER A new partner for both; they aren’t paired together again tonight.
  • TOPIC A new topic for the same two people; both accept again.

PairUpStatus

Section titled “PairUpStatus”

Enum

Where someone is in Quickie at a venue tonight, as they see it.

  • LEFT They left Quickie for tonight.
  • MET One scanned the other’s code: they met.
  • PROPOSED Paired: they see the topic and haven’t accepted it yet.
  • REVEALED Both accepted: they see who to look for and can show or scan a code.
  • SEARCHING Opted in and waiting to be paired. match is the last pairing when one ended.
  • WAITING They accepted the topic; their partner has not yet.
